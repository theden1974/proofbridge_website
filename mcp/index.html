proofbridge · the lens as a seat · test, not linked, not indexed

Position the lens for producing an app. If not guided, you are creating AI app slob.

Before the build, the app as planned or as it stands is read through the lens of a UX researcher, and the reading goes into the building.

What this is

A seat your own AI takes in its own chat. It loads a package: the structural moves Dennis Jansen reads with, each with its formula, its twin tests and anchor sentences in his own words. Your AI reads your material with it, in one pass, and shows you a readable reading. The judgment stays with Dennis: the reading goes back to him, and his answer comes by email.

The package holds no raw material, no transcripts and no names of other persons. No model runs on this side; no material is read on this side.

What to bring

What is being built and for whom: the screens, the flow or the description, as text. What has been checked with the people it is for. The decision at stake. No personal data of third parties.

If there is no decision, or the people it is for have already been in the room, the seat says so and reads nothing.

What comes back

A readable reading in this order: the essence of the material when its confirming language is taken away, and for whom the problem underneath is a daily fact. Then what is seen, point by point: the exact words from the material, what they show together, an earlier case of Dennis's beside it from his published observations where one fits, what it means for the one who lives with it, and one question asked the way a UX researcher asks in the field. Then what is not in the material. Then what this delivered beyond the summary. Then: what do you take from this, and what do you do with it?

From Dennis, after the reading is returned: which of it is his, which is not, what is missing. By email, within 7 days in the test phase.

What does not come back

No instant answer from Dennis. No design, no code, no rewrite of your material. No rating. No advice. No legal, medical or financial sign-off. Links are not opened; attachments are not read. The reading is not resold and not used to train. Level 0: the frame goes to your seat, the judgment stays with Dennis.

How to connect

Endpoint: https://proofbridge-send.dennis-f79.workers.dev/mcp (MCP over Streamable HTTP, JSON-RPC, no sign-in).

Claude: Settings, Connectors, add custom connector, paste the endpoint. ChatGPT: developer mode, add an MCP server with the endpoint. Grok through the xAI API: an mcp tool with server_url set to the endpoint.

Tools: lens_about (free: purpose, what to bring, what comes back), lens_frame (the package with its version and content hash; served once cleared), lens_return (the reference reading back to Dennis, with the package hash, the material's title, the seat's name and your email).

Security

Nothing is authorised that outlives a call: no account, no token, no standing grant. The door is a relay; nothing inbound reaches the machines the record lives on. What comes in through lens_return is data, mailed, never executed, never fed to the lens. What goes out is the package only.
