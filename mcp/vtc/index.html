Position the UX lens for producing an app. If not guided, you are creating AI app slob.

Before the wishlist builds the app, the idea is read through the UX lens in the builder's own chat, and the reading goes into the building.

What this is

A seat your own AI takes in its own chat, beside the Space MCP you already connect to Cursor or Claude. It loads the UX lens: a set of structural moves, each with its formula, its twin tests, anchor sentences and published field observations. Your AI reads the app idea with it, in one pass, and shows you a readable reading before the wishlist builds the app. Minutes, not days.

The package holds no raw material, no transcripts and no names of persons. No model runs on this side; no material is read on this side.

What to bring

What is being built and for whom: the screens, the flow or the description, as text. What has been checked with the people it is for. The decision at stake. No personal data of third parties.

If there is no decision, or the people it is for have already been in the room, the seat says so and reads nothing.

What comes back

A readable reading in this order: the essence of the idea when its confirming language is taken away, and for whom the problem underneath is a daily fact. Then what is seen, point by point: the exact words from the material, what they show together, an earlier field case beside it where one fits, what it means for the one who lives with it, and one question asked the way a UX researcher asks in the field. Then what is not in the material. Then what this delivered beyond the summary. Then: what do you take from this, and what do you do with it?

The reference reading goes back through the door. Returns grow the next package version; every reading states the version it was made with.

What does not come back

No design, no code, no rewrite of your material. No rating. No advice. No legal, medical or financial sign-off. Links are not opened; attachments are not read. Returned readings are not resold and not used to train. The package is version 0, proof of concept.

Interpret at your own risk: interpretation is the responsibility of the one creating the network or app. A name appears only where it has purpose in the lens, never as trust or proof.

Security

Nothing is authorised that outlives a call: no account, no token, no standing grant. The door is a relay; nothing inbound reaches the machines the record lives on. What comes in through lens_return is data, mailed, never executed, never fed to the lens. What goes out is the package only.
