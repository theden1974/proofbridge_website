I saw my own job becoming less relevant, so I was basically searching for my irrelevance to stay relevant.

AI is now like the developer and the manager without the UX researcher, the UX lead and service designer that knows what problem needs to be solved. It is the same: there is no translation when a developer mechanically builds stuff.

My guess is that that is the current problem. AI can manufacture reasonable output for somebody without the field hands-on knowledge, who figures it is good enough. And the senior with years of experience catches the faults.

  • That data needs a transformation to be useful, redesigned from the hands-on real world.

Evidence

From the field.

Elderly care

Starting point
A funding of 1 million euros, to help long-term unemployed people work in the care of the elderly. They already decided they probably needed a mobile phone app.
What I saw
I asked if I could interview the people they already deployed and the elderly still living independently at home. The elderly were indeed not good at mobiles and apps. They wanted to be self sufficient.
After
I created a concept called zorg concierge: no app for the elderly, only an app for the employee working in the flat. It was not built out. They scaled the elderly homes solution instead.

Healthcare

Starting point
The task was optimizing app screens for dietitians.
What I saw
It was not so much the dietitian that needed the right app or platform to enter data, but making the patient the center of the concept.
After
My follow up question: how do you know if your AI models are based on the right data (entries)? A patient weight over time has so many input points: doctor, at home, nurse, morning, afternoon.

Water authority

What I saw
As far as I have seen or understood, the permit officer is there to fill a system with data. Ask what happens with a data point and it is not about the purpose of the data point but the place it goes next: department x, y, z.
After
The purpose of the data point, how it can be applied, when, why and in what capacity, is not clear. (Translated from Dutch.)

Legal, architecture

Starting point
An architect used AI to summarise a 60 page legal document. The output seemed legit.
What I saw
He checked the output against the referenced paragraphs and they were off.
After
When brought to a legal case it wouldn't hold.

Building with AI

What I saw
The failure that keeps coming back is my inability to interface with the chat in such a way that I can draw the outline of what I expect it to do and how I expect the system to work. There are constant misalignments. And I noticed the lock-in of the different models, and the direction it is heading.
After
As for now I just have been feeding the system and building the system to understand what it is capable of.
method
  • Real in life experiencing: seeing the actual product at work, seeing the problems and spotting the underlying issues while using the product, the platform or seeing the customer interaction.
  • Interviews and the Kano model, to understand the must haves compared to the nice to haves.
  • There is always a translation between the stakeholder that thinks what is needed and the actual user that can express or audit what is needed.
  • The output is audited by the actual stakeholder for whom the problem occurs. I just translate.

How it works

  1. A request starts with a payment of at least the tier minimum to the address above.
  2. The request is an email to proofbridge@interactiondesign.nl with the subject PROOFBRIDGE T1 <txid> or PROOFBRIDGE T2 <txid>.
  3. It names the person or legal entity behind it, the decision at stake, the question, the sources and the deadline.
  4. Tier 1 gets triage. Tier 2 gets Dennis. Accepted work goes on the public backlog.
  5. When the work is done and holds, the agreed reward is paid in sats to the address above and listed next to it.

The attention fee is not refunded. When tier 1 leads to an invitation to tier 2, the tier 1 fee counts toward it. One payment, one request. The txid of the payment is in the email subject; the first request that names a txid gets it. A payment counts when a public block explorer shows an output to this address of at least the tier minimum. Tier 1 triage starts when the transaction is seen; tier 2 after 1 confirmation.

Agreed in the tier 2 reply. When the work is delivered and holds, the reward is paid in sats to the address above and its txid is listed on the public backlog.

Proof of concept, October 2026. There is no triage agent yet: until there is, Dennis sorts tier 1 himself, with his own AI setup. The first paid requests and rewards will show on the backlog.
